# SCOUT Cohort 1

Welcome to the official repository for **SCOUT Cohort 1**.

This repository serves as the central learning and project space for the cohort. It contains class materials, practical assignments, exercises, projects, and other resources that support the learning journey.

SCOUT is designed to help learners develop practical skills, apply their knowledge to real-world problems, create meaningful impact, and position themselves for new opportunities.

---

## The SCOUT Journey

### Springboard

Build the knowledge, practical skills, confidence, and foundation needed to take your next step.

### Impact

Apply what you learn to real-world problems, build meaningful projects, conduct research, and create evidence of your capabilities.

### Opportunity

Use your skills, projects, portfolio, research, and network to position yourself for academic, professional, and career opportunities.

**Springboard. Impact. Opportunity.**

## Portfolio Development

As part of the cohort, learners will create and publish a personal portfolio landing page.

The portfolio may include:

- About Me
- Skills
- Projects
- Education
- Certifications
- Achievements
- Research Experience
- Contact Information
- Professional Profiles

Learners will also be introduced to **GitHub Pages** for publishing their portfolio online.

The objective is for every learner to have a professional online space where they can document their work, demonstrate their skills, and position themselves for opportunities.

##Learning Approach

SCOUT emphasizes practical learning and application.

Learners are expected to:

- Attend scheduled sessions.
- Participate in practical exercises.
- Complete assigned tasks.
- Build projects.
- Document their work.
- Collaborate with other learners.
- Seek and apply feedback.
- Build and maintain their professional portfolio.
- Repository Guidelines

This repository is a shared learning space for SCOUT Cohort 1.

Please:

- Keep your work organized.
- Follow the instructions provided for each activity.
- Use clear and meaningful file names.
- Write meaningful commit messages.
- Commit your work regularly.
- Do not delete or modify another learner's work.
- Respect other learners and their contributions.
- Ask for assistance when needed.
